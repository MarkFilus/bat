    how this was cooked

    methodology

    listed venue municipalities were grouped into u.s. census metropolitan statistical areas, then divided by july 1, 2025 metro population estimates.

    1 million residents
    minimum venue count
    2 listed venues

    important caveat

    this ranks venues documented in an open, volunteer-maintained database—not every operating imax screen. counts change, classifications are messy, and “realness” remains peer-review resistant.
